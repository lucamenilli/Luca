Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Purpose

Jarvis rclone is a personal application used to access and manage my own Google Drive accounts through rclone on my local computer.

Google Drive Data

The application accesses Google Drive files and metadata only to provide file access and management functionality requested by the account owner.

Data Storage

rclone may store authentication credentials, file metadata, cached file contents and diagnostic logs locally on the user's computer.

Data Sharing

This application is not offered to external users and does not sell or share Google Drive data with third parties.

Access Revocation

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
